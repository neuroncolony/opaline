How Lustre is built

View raw audit JSON

Non-custodial: every trade is signed in your wallet and no Lustre contract ever holds funds. There are no accounts; orders, alerts and auto-invest plans are stored only in your browser. Swaps route through LI.FI, and market data comes from CoinGecko and DefiLlama.
