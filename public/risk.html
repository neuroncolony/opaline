Risk Disclosure

Last updated October 1, 2026

Market risk

The value of tokenized stocks, ETFs, commodities and treasuries can fall as well as rise, and past performance does not predict future results.

Issuer risk

Each token is issued by a third party that holds or references the underlying asset. Redemption rights, backing, fees and eligibility differ between issuers and depend on their solvency and operations.

Liquidity and price differences

On-chain liquidity can be thin. A token may trade away from the price of its underlying asset, especially outside traditional market hours.

Smart contract and bridge risk

Transactions rely on smart contracts, bridges and blockchains that may contain bugs, be exploited, or experience congestion and outages.

Regulatory risk

Rules for tokenized securities vary by country and may change. Some assets may become restricted or unavailable to you.

Irreversible transactions

Blockchain transactions are final. Sending to a wrong address or approving a malicious contract can result in permanent loss.

No advice

Nothing on Lustre is investment, legal or tax advice. Consider your own circumstances and seek independent advice where appropriate.
