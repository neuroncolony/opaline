Privacy Policy

Last updated October 1, 2026

Overview

Lustre is built to work without accounts. We do not ask for your name, email or identity documents to use the interface.

Data stored in your browser

Your watchlist, alerts, baskets, theme and similar preferences are stored locally in your browser. They never leave your device unless you clear or export them.

Wallet addresses

When you connect a wallet, your public address is used in your browser to read balances and build transactions. Addresses you paste into Portfolio are queried against public blockchain data and are not stored by us.

Server logs

Our hosting provider keeps standard technical logs such as IP address, user agent and request time for security and reliability. These are retained for a limited period and are not sold.

Third parties

Market data, routing and price requests may be sent to third-party providers to serve the page. Their own privacy policies apply.

Cookies

Lustre does not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Your choices

You can clear all locally stored data at any time from your browser settings. For any privacy request, contact privacy@lustre.markets.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest revision.
