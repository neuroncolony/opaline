What Opaline is

Opaline is a marketplace for tokenized real-world assets: stocks, ETFs, gold, treasuries and private credit, issued on public blockchains by regulated and unregulated issuers alike. It brings every issuer onto one screen, prices them live, and routes trades across chains.

Opaline is not a custodian and not a broker. It never holds your assets, never asks for a deposit, and adds no fee of its own. When you buy, tokens go straight to your wallet. When you sell, they go straight from it.

How routing works

Every trade is routed through LI.FI, an aggregator that sits on top of dozens of bridges and decentralized exchanges. When you press buy, Opaline asks the aggregator for the cheapest path from what you hold to the asset you want. You see the full quote: the amount in, the amount out, the route, the fees and the gas. One signature submits the whole thing.

If you pay with a token the destination contract has not seen before, an approval transaction is requested first. That is standard for ERC-20 tokens: approve the router to spend the exact amount, then sign the swap. Native gas tokens need no approval.

After signing, the trade is tracked until the destination chain confirms. The status appears in your recent activity with a link to the explorer.

Supported chains and issuers

Opaline routes across every chain the aggregator supports, more than twenty EVM networks plus Solana. Assets from every major issuer are included: tokenized stock wrappers, ETF mantles, gold tokens, treasury tokens and private credit pools.

Buying and selling

Pick an asset anywhere in the app and press buy. The trade sheet opens with a quote in about a second. Choose what to pay with: any token you hold on any supported chain. Slippage is configurable, and the route is shown before you sign.

Selling works the same way in reverse. If the same asset trades on several chains, you can buy on one chain and sell on another; the bridge hop is part of the route.

Every quote is a firm estimate at sign time. If the price moves past your slippage before confirmation, the trade reverts rather than filling at a worse price.

Baskets and sharing

A basket is a named set of assets with weights. Build one, save it, and buy the whole thing in one pass: Opaline splits the amount by weight and runs one trade per asset. The agent can build one from a sentence, like build a basket of NVDA, AAPL and gold.

Baskets can be shared. A share link encodes the assets and weights so anyone can preview it and buy the same mix. Shared baskets are content, not funds: nothing moves until someone decides to buy.

Strategies

Strategies turn a market view into a repeatable action. The three Opaline ships with are limit orders (buy or sell at a price you choose), auto-invest plans (recurring buys on a schedule), and price alerts (get told when a line is crossed). All three run locally and are covered below.

Limit orders, alerts and auto-invest

These three features run entirely in your browser. Your orders, alerts and plans are stored in local storage, checked against live prices while Opaline is open, and never sent to a server.

  • Limit orders. You set a price and a size. When the market crosses your limit, the order is marked triggered and you get a notification. You then press Fill now and sign the trade yourself.
  • Price alerts. Above, below, or a percentage move in 24 hours. When one fires you get a notification, and it waits for you to re-arm it.
  • Auto-invest. A plan buys your assets on a rhythm: daily, weekly, biweekly or monthly. Each run opens the trade sheet for you to sign. Pause, edit or delete any time.

Because nothing executes without your signature, a closed tab means nothing fills. Open Opaline when it matters: that is the trade for keeping custody of your own keys.

Yield and lending data sources

Yield and lending figures come from DefiLlama, which indexes on-chain pools and money markets. Opaline shows APY, TVL, the project and the chain for each pool, and links to the underlying data. The figures are point-in-time reads of on-chain reality, not promises: rates on-chain move constantly.

Portfolio tracking

The portfolio page reads balances across every supported chain, either from your connected wallet or from any address you paste in. Tokenized assets are recognized by their contract addresses and matched to live market prices, so real-world positions are valued with the market, not with stale marks.

Everything is read only. Looking at a portfolio never requires a signature, and a watched address is stored only in your browser.

The Agent

The agent turns sentences into market answers. The built-in engine runs in your browser and handles a fixed set of commands: prices, issuer comparisons, top movers, best yields, basket building, price alerts and trades. Type help on the agent page for the full list.

For anything outside that list, you can supply your own model key in the agent settings. The key is stored only in your browser's local storage, and requests go directly from your browser to the provider. Opaline never sees it. Without a key, the built-in engine still covers every command above.

Self custody and security

Your keys stay in your wallet, full stop. Opaline never asks for a seed phrase, never holds a balance, and never takes custody mid-trade: the router moves assets from your wallet to your wallet in one transaction.

Opaline charges zero fees of its own. What you pay is gas plus the bridge or exchange fee inside the route, and every quote shows that breakdown before you sign. Sign only what you have read. An approval should always match the amount you intend to trade.

Data sources

  • Prices, market caps, volume, supply: CoinGecko public API, refreshed continuously.
  • Routing, bridges, chains, balances: LI.FI aggregation API.
  • Pool and pair discovery: DexScreener.
  • Yield and lending rates: DefiLlama.

All four are read as public data. Nothing you do on Opaline sends your keys, your wallet address or your orders to any of them beyond the calls needed to serve the page you are on.
